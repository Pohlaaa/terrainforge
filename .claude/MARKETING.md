# TerrainForge — Marketing & Customer Acquisition

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
**Primary:** Owner-operator of a landscaping company with 2–15 employees, $250K–$2M annual revenue, doing design+install work (not just maintenance). Tech-comfortable but not tech-obsessed. Pain: quoting takes too long, materials get wasted, crew doesn't know what to do without constant check-ins.

**Secondary:** Operations manager at a larger landscaping company (15–50 employees) who is tired of managing projects via spreadsheet + text message.

**Not a fit:** Pure maintenance companies (mowing/blowing), residential DIY, companies with 1 person.

## Value Propositions (in order of resonance)
1. **"Quote faster, waste less"** — Generate a material manifest from a project description in minutes, not hours. Reserve percentages built in so you don't over-order.
2. **"Your crew knows what to do"** — Work orders auto-generated from the manifest. No more calling the foreman 5 times per job.
3. **"Stop losing money on materials"** — AI-powered price research shows you if you're overpaying. Inventory tracking catches waste.
4. **"Everything in one place"** — Projects, crew, equipment, materials — not split across 4 apps and 2 spreadsheets.

## Messaging Rules
- Talk like a contractor, not like a tech startup. Say "job" not "project." Say "crew" not "team." Say "quote" not "estimate."
- Lead with pain, not features. "Tired of doing your manifest by hand?" not "Introducing the ManifestEngine™"
- Use numbers when possible: "Save 3 hours per quote", "Reduce material waste by 15%"
- Avoid buzzwords: "AI-powered" is fine, "revolutionary", "game-changing", "seamless" are not

## Acquisition Channels (Phase 1)
In order of priority and cost-effectiveness:

### 1. Direct Outreach (Week 1–8, $0)
LinkedIn + cold email to landscaping company owners. 10 outreaches/day. Goal: book demo calls.
Message framework: Pain → Curiosity → CTA
"Most landscaping companies I talk to spend 3+ hours doing material lists by hand for every project. We built something that does it in 5 minutes. Worth a 15-min demo?"

### 2. Facebook/Instagram Groups (Week 1–, $0)
Join landscaping contractor groups (Landscape Contractor Talk, NALP Facebook groups). Provide value first (answer questions). Mention TerrainForge when relevant. No spamming.

### 3. Landscaping Industry Forums & Reddit ($0)
r/landscaping, Lawn Site forums. Same approach — value first, mention the product when directly relevant.

### 4. Paid Social (Month 3+, once product is stable)
Facebook/Instagram ads targeting landscaping businesses. Start at $20/day, test 3 creatives.
Best performing creative types (for tools/SaaS): before/after workflow, screen recording demo, testimonial.

### 5. SEO Content (Month 2+, $0 cost, time investment)
Target keywords: "landscaping estimate software", "landscaping material calculator", "landscaping project management app"
Content format: how-to guides, comparison posts, template downloads (free material list template → capture email)

## Demo Playbook
When doing a live demo (video call or in-person), follow this flow:

**1. Discover first (5 min):** "How do you currently do your material lists? How long does it take? What's your biggest frustration with job management right now?"

**2. Show the pain point they mentioned (10 min):** If they said quoting takes too long → show the manifest engine. If they said crew coordination → show work orders. Don't show everything.

**3. The "5-minute manifest" demo:** Take a real project they're working on. Describe it out loud. Show the manifest generate. Show the cost rollup. This is the highest-converting moment.

**4. Handle objections:**
- "We use Excel" → "This does what Excel does but with material quantities pre-calculated and crew packets auto-generated. Want me to show you the manifest for the job you just described?"
- "Too expensive" → Start with Starter tier, frame as less than 1 hour of your time per month
- "Not tech-savvy" → "If you can use your phone to text, you can use this. Let me show you the work order screen."

**5. Close:** "Want to start a free trial this week? I can have your account set up in 10 minutes."
